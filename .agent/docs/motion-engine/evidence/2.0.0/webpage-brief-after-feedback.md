# Creative brief
Revision: 4
Status: awaiting-agreement

## Confirmed requirements
Create an impressive, engaging exploding/animated webpage explaining processes for web design, ecommerce and on-page SEO. Design and SEO must work together from the beginning, not appear as three services added in sequence. Lots of individual parts move continuously, like an exploded watch; sliding panels alone are insufficient. Private presentation is acceptable. Earlier creative work was reset.
Project context: small agency homepage for business owners, charcoal and orange. Static HTML; local-only work, no external services, paid generation, installs, deployment or repository operations.

## Revised proposal: One website, working together
A suspended, intricate website mechanism is visible from the opening. Around 40 independently moving elements represent the actual work: search-intent markers, headings, navigation links, text lines, images, product attributes, price, basket components, buttons and measurement marks. Design and SEO elements are connected and moving together immediately. Ecommerce belongs to the same mechanism. The watch reference informs detailed exploded spatial relationships and continuous coordinated movement, not literal decorative gears.

Scroll changes the spacing and viewpoint around the same mechanism. It reveals three shared stages: understand customers (search questions shape content and structure), shape the experience (headings, navigation and product information develop together), then test and refine (readability, purchasing and page checks). All disciplines remain visible throughout; attention changes without adding a new service as a separate layer. Illustrative relationships need not imply verified proprietary agency methods.

The signature moment expands the system deeply into space: tiny parts continue to rotate, orbit or travel short paths while connected elements move in synchrony. It contracts into a readable desktop/mobile website while motion remains visible around its edges. Visitors leave understanding that design, commerce and search are planned together and can reach a project-enquiry invitation.

Charcoal, orange, readable typography and precise mechanical movement support the scene. Explanatory text stays still and outside the moving mechanism. Hover/focus can identify selected parts; scrolling controls the overall progression while small continuous movement runs independently. Provide an explicit pause control and a static reduced-motion equivalent with all explanations. Mobile preserves distinct parts and relationships with a less dense composition.

Content and website examples are illustrative. No real client assets, agency-specific step details or enquiry destination have been supplied. Avoid invented results and ranking promises. Implementation route is undecided; choose after agreement, within local/no-service constraints. If those constraints materially limit the agreed motion, raise that specific trade-off.
